About Market Soleil

Market Soleil L.L.C. is a Caribbean and Afro-diaspora company based in Madison, Wisconsin. Founded with deep roots in Haitian culture, we operate at the intersection of food, culture, and technology.

On the retail side, we bring authentic Caribbean products, spices, and cultural goods to our community. On the technology side, we build innovative iOS applications — including AI-powered meal planning tools inspired by Caribbean and global cuisines, and multilingual wellness apps serving diverse communities.

Our name, "Soleil" — French for sun — reflects the warmth, energy, and vibrancy of the Caribbean culture that inspires everything we do.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 10, 2026

Market Soleil L.L.C. ("we," "our," or "us") is a Caribbean and Afro-diaspora company that operates retail food products and mobile applications including Tonight's Menu, First Breath, and Daily Dad. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our mobile apps.

Information We Collect

Camera & Photo Access
Tonight's Menu may request access to your camera or photo library to scan ingredients. Photos are processed in real-time to identify ingredients and are not stored on our servers after processing.

Health Data
First Breath integrates with Apple HealthKit to store breathing session data. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Usage Data
We collect anonymous usage data to improve our apps, including features used, app performance metrics, and crash reports.

Purchase Information
Subscriptions and purchases are processed securely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment details.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide AI-powered ingredient detection and recipe suggestions
  • Deliver guided breathing exercises and track sessions
  • Improve our apps and services
  • Process subscriptions

Third-Party Services

  • OpenAI — For AI-powered image analysis in Tonight's Menu. Photos are processed but not stored or used for AI training.
  • RevenueCat — For subscription management.
  • Apple App Store — For payment processing.
  • Apple HealthKit — For storing breathing session data locally (First Breath only).

Data Storage & Security

  • Photos are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored
  • Saved meals, preferences, and health data are stored locally on your device
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data

Your Rights

  • Delete locally stored data by uninstalling the app
  • Opt out of analytics through your device settings
  • Cancel subscriptions at any time through the App Store
  • Control Health data sharing through iOS Settings

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Tonight's Menu

How do I scan my fridge?

Open the app, tap the Scan tab, then use "Take Photo" or "Photo Library" to capture an image of your ingredients. Our AI will identify them and suggest matching recipes.

How many free scans do I get?

You get 5 free AI scans. After that, upgrade to Premium for unlimited scans and full access to all features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Tonight's Menu → Cancel. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, navigate to the upgrade screen, and tap "Restore Purchases."

First Breath

What languages are supported?

First Breath offers voice guidance in English, French, Spanish, and Haitian Creole.

Does First Breath work with Apple Health?

Yes. First Breath can save your breathing sessions to Apple HealthKit. You can manage this in iOS Settings → Health.
